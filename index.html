Questions

Why is it only $300?

Because we keep the scope focused.

We specialize in one-page HVAC landing pages rather than large custom websites with weeks of planning and unnecessary features. That helps us keep the process efficient and the price straightforward.

Do I need to replace my existing website?

No.

A landing page can work alongside your current site. You don't necessarily need to move away from Squarespace, WordPress, Webflow, or your existing booking software. We'll recommend the simplest suitable setup.

What if I already use Jobber or another booking tool?

That's fine.

We can link to your existing booking process or connect supported forms and workflows where practical. More advanced integrations may require a separate quote.

Do I need to be running Google Ads?

No.

Your landing page can also be used in social media profiles, referral campaigns, emails, or other places customers discover your business. If you're running ads, we can make the page relevant to the service you're promoting.

Can I edit the page myself?

That depends on where we build it.

We can discuss an easy-to-manage setup before starting, so you know exactly what you'll be able to update yourself. Or you can let us handle routine updates through monthly care.

Can you guarantee more calls or jobs?

No honest web developer can guarantee that.

The results depend on how many people visit your page, what services you offer, your market, and how inquiries are handled.

What we can do is make the page easy to navigate, make contacting you straightforward, and set up the agreed tracking so you have better visibility into customer inquiries.

How quickly can the page go live?

For a standard page, we aim for seven business days once we've received the required information, assets, and access.

More complex requirements may take longer, and we'll agree on that before starting.

Do I need the $70 monthly plan?

No.

The landing page is a one-time project. Monthly care is completely optional.

What happens if something doesn't work after launch?

We'll correct defects in the agreed implementation that are identified during the included post-launch support period, at no additional charge.

We'll explain the support period and scope before work begins.
